# Sword combat sounds

Author: **Hove Audio**.
Pack: **Sword Combat Sound Effects Pack Free Version**.
Source: https://hoveaudio.itch.io/sword-combat-sound-effects-pack-free-version
Downloaded by the project owner and supplied as `Sword Combat Sound Effects Pack FREE VERSION.zip`.

The author's page labels the pack Royalty Free and states in the comments that credit is not required.
These recordings remain the work of Hove Audio; they are not original project recordings or CC0 assets.

Selected separate layers (not the pre-mixed combo files):

- `WHSH_Whoosh_*_07/26`: sword swing, including misses.
- `GOREStab_SwordStabGore_*_01/11/17`: flesh impacts.
- `WEAPSwrd_BaseMetal_*_03/06`: armour, stone, ice and other hard surfaces (a stylized metallic impact).

Project edits: convert to stereo PCM16 WAV at 44.1 kHz, trim leading silence and quiet tails,
normalize peaks below clipping, add short fades. Original source file names are retained.
Player hurt/death cues also use the pack's supplied damage grunts (`01`, `13`, `27`).

Additional edited variations are generated by `java tools/PrepareSoundVariations.java`:

- `demon_*`: lower-pitched dry flesh impact.
- `slime_*`: filtered, modulated wet impact with short reflections.
- `mystic_*`: lighter flesh/air transient with a quiet metallic accent.
- `ghost_*`: layered forward/reversed whoosh with stereo reflections and a long reverb tail; no flesh recording.
- `fire_*`: whoosh mixed with deterministic synthesized noise/crackles.
- `poison_*`: synthesized descending bubble tones with a quiet wet layer.

These are project edits and layers based on the supplied recordings, not additional downloaded libraries.
Each group contains two variations, normalized below clipping with short fades.

Playback uses separate swing and actual-hit events. There is no impact on misses, zero damage,
or diagnostic low-HP setup. Random sample variations avoid immediate repetition where possible.

Event cues are generated reproducibly with `java tools/PrepareEventSounds.java`:

- `player_hurt_0/1`, `player_death`: Hove Audio damage grunts from the owner's supplied ZIP, converted,
  trimmed and normalized; the death grunt is lowered in pitch.
- `jump`, `dodge`, `summon`, `stone_cast`, `ice_cast`: short edited air layers from the supplied whoosh,
  with project-generated tones or thuds where applicable.
- `land`, `enemy_down`, `boss_down`, `wave_start`, `wave_clear`, `stage_clear`, `level_up`, `potion`,
  `phase_change`: original deterministic synthesized tones/noise, without external recordings.

All event WAV files are stereo PCM16 at 44.1 kHz, with short fades and peaks below clipping.
Movement cues are quiet; hurt sounds occur only on actual HP loss. Fatal blows use the death voice
instead of overlapping two grunts. Campaign audio persists across the transition to the market so
the level-up cue can finish.
